THE BRIEF

From $3K/mo to $48K/mo in 14 months.

Dave had been running his house washing business for 4 years on word of mouth and a Facebook page that got the odd "boost". He'd plateau'd at around $3k/month and was driving 2 hours each way to quote jobs that paid $400.

We rebuilt his entire pipeline: tight geo-targeted Meta ads pointing at a custom landing page, a qualifier that ruled out anyone outside a 45-minute radius or under $600 budget, and a booking flow that put quotes straight in his calendar.

Six weeks in he was profitable. Six months in he hired his first employee. Today, he's got two crews, an 8-week wait list, and his weekends back.
