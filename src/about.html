ABOUT / THE STORY

G'day.
I'm Marcus.

My old man's been a concreter in Brisbane for 30 years. Growing up, I watched him spend Sundays driving across town to quote jobs that never landed.

He's a craftsman. He shouldn't have to be a marketer too.

So in 2021 I started Marcus Results. We do one thing: bring qualified jobs to home-services trades. No fluff, no funnels, no jargon. Just the right kind of phone calls.
