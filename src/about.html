ABOUT / THE STORY

G'day.
I'm Marcus.

I studied marketing at QUT. It taught me how people take in a message. And how they decide who to trust. It also showed me where businesses fall down most. They struggle to get the message right. They struggle to build trust. So I set out to help with both.

Then I saw what agencies were doing. The ad said one thing. The page said another. Each might be fine on its own. But the ad's promise didn't carry through to the page. Trust broke at the moment it mattered. The click happened. The job didn't.

That bothered me. So I dug into how people decide. The answer came from Eugene Schwartz and his five stages of awareness. Meet people where their head is at, and selling gets easier.

So I built an approach where the ad and the page tell one story. Trust carries through from click to call.
